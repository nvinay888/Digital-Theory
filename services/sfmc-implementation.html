FAQ

Frequently asked questions about salesforce marketing cloud implementation

What is Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC)?

SFMC is Salesforce's enterprise marketing platform for cross-channel engagement — email, SMS, push, WhatsApp and in-app — powered by Journey Builder, a flexible data model and Einstein AI personalisation.

What does Digitaltheory's SFMC implementation include?

Audit and strategy, data model and ingestion, journey design and build, personalisation and Einstein AI, reporting and attribution, and training plus an optional managed-service for ongoing ops.

How long until SFMC pays back?

With the right data model and priority journeys built first, SFMC typically pays back in months rather than years — because value comes from how it is implemented, not just the licence.

Which journeys do you build first?

Usually the highest-value lifecycle journeys — welcome, browse-abandon, cart-abandon, post-purchase, win-back and VIP — orchestrated across email, push, WhatsApp, SMS and in-app.

Do you offer ongoing SFMC support?

Yes. Beyond enablement for your in-house team, we offer a managed-service option covering ongoing campaign operations, QA and continuous improvement.
