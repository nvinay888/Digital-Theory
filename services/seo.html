FAQ

Frequently asked questions about seo

What SEO services does Digitaltheory offer?

Technical SEO and site audits, programmatic SEO, an editorial content engine, link building and digital PR, and local and international SEO — reported in the same dashboard as your paid channels.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO compounds over months rather than days. Technical fixes can show quickly, while content and links typically build organic share-of-search over 3-6 months and beyond, steadily lowering blended CAC.

How does SEO reduce customer acquisition cost?

Organic traffic has no per-click cost, so growing organic share-of-search lowers your blended CAC. We report share-of-search, blended CAC and organic-attributed revenue in one view.

Do you do technical SEO and Core Web Vitals?

Yes. Our technical audits cover crawlability, indexation, schema, Core Web Vitals and information architecture — the foundation content and links are built on.

What SEO results have you delivered?

For Pocket52, combined ASO and content work lifted organic installs by 20%. Our deliverables include a technical audit, a content engine with topic clusters and cadence, a link program, and clear reporting.
