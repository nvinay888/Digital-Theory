FAQ

Frequently asked questions about sap business one implementation

What is SAP Business One (SAP B1)?

SAP Business One is an ERP for small and mid-sized businesses that unifies finance, sales, CRM, inventory, purchasing, MRP and analytics in one system — the operating spine for a company that has outgrown spreadsheets.

What does Digitaltheory's SAP B1 implementation include?

Discovery and blueprint, configuration and customisation, data migration, integrations (Shopify, Amazon, banks, payment gateways, WhatsApp/CRM, BI), plus training, change management and AMC.

How long does SAP B1 implementation take?

Well-scoped implementations can go live in weeks rather than months, phased by module. Timelines depend on process complexity, data quality and the number of integrations.

Can SAP B1 integrate with Shopify and Amazon?

Yes. We connect SAP B1 to Shopify, Amazon and other marketplaces, banks, payment gateways and BI dashboards via the DI-API or service layer, so orders, inventory and finance stay in sync.

Do you provide support after go-live?

Yes. Our hyper-care and AMC covers post-go-live support, version upgrades, query resolution and continuous improvement against your operating KPIs, so adoption sticks.
