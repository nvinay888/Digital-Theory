FAQ

Frequently asked questions about app development

What kind of apps does Digitaltheory build?

We build native iOS and Android apps and cross-platform React Native apps for consumer businesses — with ASO, in-app analytics and event-driven CRM baked in from day one so the app is a growth engine.

What is ASO and do you offer it?

ASO (app store optimisation) improves your app's visibility and install rate in the App Store and Play Store through keyword research, creative refresh and rank monitoring. It is included in our app engagements.

How do you improve app retention and LTV?

We design a shared event taxonomy across product, performance and CRM, then wire push and in-app messaging to behaviour. For Laundrokart this helped drive a 55% installs lift and 23% LTV improvement.

How much does it cost to build an app in India?

Cost depends on platforms (native vs cross-platform), features and integrations. We scope on a discovery call and price around the outcome — share your product goals for a tailored estimate.

What deliverables are included?

Product and UX (flows, prototypes, research), native or cross-platform engineering with store submissions, a shared event-taxonomy analytics schema, and an ongoing ASO program.
