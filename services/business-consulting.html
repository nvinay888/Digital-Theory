How we work

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about our consulting practice

What does a Digitaltheory consulting engagement look like?

Every engagement runs on a four-phase operating model: Diagnose (two weeks inside your data), Hypothesise (a one-page memo and a working financial model), Run the play (six to twelve weeks of experiments shipped alongside your team) and Operate (quarterly cadence where the model updates with reality).

Do you write recommendations or ship them?

Both. Every phase ends with a working artefact — a model, a memo, a tested campaign or a launched SKU. We don't do slide-only deliverables.

Which consumer sub-sectors do you cover?

D2C & e-commerce, beauty & personal care, fashion & apparel, food & beverages, consumer electronics, consumer health, edtech and multi-branch retail. Patterns travel across sub-sectors; specifics don't. We bring both.

What's a typical engagement size and duration?

Diagnostic sprints are 4 weeks. Strategy & turnaround engagements are 12 weeks. Market-entry pilots are 8 weeks (country prioritisation to live pilot). Retained advisory is quarterly on-call counsel for leadership teams.

Do you help with M&A due diligence?

Yes. We run buy-side commercial due diligence, target scanning, synergy modelling and post-deal integration — using the same operating chassis we use for organic growth engagements.

How is pricing structured?

Outcome-driven pricing tied to the metric that matters to the P&L — CAC, CM3, LTV, category share or NPD payback. Engagements are scoped around the result, not the hours.
