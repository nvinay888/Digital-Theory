FAQ

Common questions about working with us

How quickly do you respond to enquiries?

We reply to every enquiry within one business day. Discovery calls are usually scheduled within the same week.

What does a Digitaltheory engagement look like?

Every engagement follows a four-step operating cadence: Discovery call → Strategy → Resource plan → Biweekly updates. We scope around the outcome you want to move, not a fixed list of deliverables.

Do you work with our existing MarTech, ERP or CRM stack?

Yes. We integrate with your existing Shopify, Amazon, HubSpot, Salesforce, Klaviyo, GA4, ERP or CDP — no forced replatforming. Where a tool is missing, we recommend the smallest incremental spend that closes the gap.

What's the smallest engagement you take on?

A four-week diagnostic sprint that ends in a prioritised, one-page plan and a working financial model. Most clients start there and expand into a strategy & turnaround engagement afterwards.

Where are you based and which markets do you cover?

Offices in Bengaluru and Mumbai. We run engagements across India, GCC (UAE, Saudi Arabia), Southeast Asia and select US brands.

How do you price consulting and performance engagements?

Outcome-driven pricing tied to the metric that matters — CAC, ROAS, CM3, LTV or category share. We win when your business does.
