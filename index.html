FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Digitaltheory do?

Digitaltheory is a data-first growth marketing company. We help brands scale profitably through performance marketing, branding, SEO, web & app development, business consulting, CRM & retention, and platform implementations such as SAP Business One and Salesforce Marketing Cloud.

Which industries do you work with?

D2C & e-commerce, beauty & personal care, edtech, consumer apps, real-money gaming, retail & pharma, fintech and B2B SaaS. Featured brands include Groww, upGrad, Pepperfry, Celio, Codingal, Pocket52, Laundrokart, Nutriglow and Thulasi Pharmacy.

How does Digitaltheory price engagements?

Outcome-driven pricing tied to the metric that matters — CAC, ROAS, CM3, LTV or category share. Engagements are scoped around the business result, not hours or retainers.

What sizes of engagement do you take on?

From focused 4-week diagnostic sprints through 12-week strategy engagements to yearly performance and consulting programs at ₹1 Cr–₹5 Cr media budgets. We manage over ₹100 Cr in cumulative ad spend across our client base.

How do you operate with an existing team?

We plug in alongside in-house teams via a four-step operating rhythm: Discovery call → Strategy → Resource plan → Biweekly updates. We integrate with your existing MarTech, ERP, CRM and CDP stack — no forced replatforming.

Where are Digitaltheory's offices?

Bengaluru and Mumbai, India. We run engagements across India, GCC (UAE, Saudi Arabia), Southeast Asia and select US brands.
